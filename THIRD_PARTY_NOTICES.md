# Third-party asset notices

This file records provenance information for model weights fetched into `app/src/main/assets/models/` by [scripts/fetch_models.py](scripts/fetch_models.py), and for fonts bundled by the Android app. Model sizes and SHA-256 values are pinned in [scripts/models.manifest](scripts/models.manifest); the hashes identify exact bytes but do not by themselves establish redistribution permission. See [docs/MODEL_SOURCES.md](docs/MODEL_SOURCES.md) for model source, conversion, and license details. The root application license is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

PaddleOCR and the PP-OCRv6 model cards declare Apache-2.0. Pinned upstream links for the PP-OCRv6 recognition model, detector model, configuration, and character dictionary are recorded in [docs/MODEL_SOURCES.md](docs/MODEL_SOURCES.md) and `scripts/models.manifest`.
